# 06 — Stop instructing the retry, and name the outcome when no round can complete

Status: ready-for-agent

**Blocked by:** 03 — the reason code is the fact this prose leaves the orchestrator to judge on.

**What to build:** nothing in the delivery skill pushes the **orchestrator** to re-dispatch a round whose failure it
can see is deterministic, and the end state it had to invent has a name. In an observed run the skill said a failed
round "is not one of the two — dispatch `code-reviewer` again" and "when the count is in doubt, spend a round"; the
orchestrator stopped at four rounds despite that text, after 5h51m and 6,256,489 billable tokens, and then invented
"leave it a draft and report the review stage as a **hand-off**" because stage 7 defines no outcome for it. Settled as
D11, D12 and D13 in `../spec.md`.

- [ ] The imperative to dispatch `code-reviewer` again after a `failed` or `cancelled` round is gone.
- [ ] The two facts standing beside the deleted imperatives are kept: a fresh dispatch opens a fresh round under a
      fresh id, and a resumed run cannot read off how many rounds already ran. **Resume** sends the reader here for why
      `code-reviewer` is the one agent a re-dispatch is not safe for, and those two sentences are the whole answer.
- [ ] "When the count is in doubt, spend a round" is gone.
- [ ] The bar is unchanged: two rounds that reached `completed`, and a round that produced no review is not one of the
      two. What guards it in the sentence being cut is kept — flipping ready on one round ships a review nobody did.
- [ ] The resumed run's fix waves no longer rest on a rule about rounds. That paragraph calls spending a wave in doubt
      "the rule **Rounds** already applies to a round"; the rule stays and its grounds become the ones the same
      paragraph already gives — a wave meeting a fully adjudicated change request replies nothing, one meeting no
      unresolved comment commits nothing.
- [ ] Nothing presumes a dead round is followed by another. The task list's rule that a dead round's retry gets a task
      of its own becomes conditional on the orchestrator spending one, which is another **dispatch** either way.
- [ ] Stage 7 names the outcome when the bar cannot be met — no round completed, or one did and the second cannot: the
      change request stays a **draft**, and the report says the review stage was handed off, with the reasons the
      rounds ended on.
- [ ] Those reasons ride beside the count the report already gives, so "how many rounds completed" says why the rest
      did not. The review stage takes no new line: the **hand-off** list already covers anything else still waiting on
      a human.
- [ ] Nothing states a round budget, a retry cap, or a retryable-versus-not classification: the codes describe, and the
      orchestrator decides.
- [ ] What the report owes about **spend** is unchanged, unknown included.
- [ ] Register and the file's prevailing column width are matched.

Three of the four defects below would have shipped the pressure with every box ticked.

- **Two of the deleted sentences carry the facts the resume rules depend on.** **Resume** says re-dispatching any agent

- **The rule is stated a second time, in words a grep for it does not find.** The paragraph on a resumed run's
