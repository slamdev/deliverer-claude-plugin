# 05 — Hand the fix wave the round's findings, posted or not

Status: ready-for-agent

**Blocked by:** 01 — the decision above this prose says the reviewer's own comments are how findings travel.

**What to build:** findings a round raised reach the **fix wave** that acts on them whether or not the reviewer posted
a comment. The round's prose rides in the `comments-addresser` **dispatch**, which is a deliberate exception to a
dispatch carrying paths rather than contents — stated where that rule is stated, or the next reader removes it as an
inconsistency. Nothing counts comments and no round's completion turns on whether findings landed. Settled as D3 and D4
in `../spec.md`.

- [ ] The prose is not a comment and nothing marks it: there is nothing to reply to and no id to name, and a mark

- [ ] The first of those lands on the agent's **Resume** paragraph, which today says **unresolved** is the whole filter

- [ ] Nothing anywhere counts comments to decide whether a round produced a review. This is a guard rather than a

- [ ] The delivery skill no longer states that the findings are already comments. It says so twice: that the tools
