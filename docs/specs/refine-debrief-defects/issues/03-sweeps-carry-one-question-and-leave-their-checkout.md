# 03 — Sweeps carry one question of fact and leave their checkout to the writers

**What to build:** sweeps that never queue behind one another, never close a fact a sibling is out to close, never
rediscover how the environment is reached, and leave every checkout they make for the writers to open. A sweep's
dispatch carries one question of fact; beside its subject, the first-hand rule and what it owes back, it carries the
subjects of the sweeps already in flight, the environment facts an earlier report established, the shared bounds by
reference to the skill's own copy, and that a checkout it makes is kept and named by path and ref in its report. The
brief lists that checkout; `spec-writer` opens the checkout paths the brief lists. The orchestrator spends no dispatch
as a timer, and the run's report lists every scratch path its sweeps and writers kept. Settled under **The refine
skill** and **`spec-writer`** in `../spec.md`; stories 8–12, 14.

**Blocked by:** 02 — The shared bounds in all three files (the sweep dispatch passes the skill's copy on by
reference).
